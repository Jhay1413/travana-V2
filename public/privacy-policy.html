Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 24, 2026

Travel Command Center ("we", "our", "us") operates the Travel Command Center application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our application.

Information We Collect

When you use our application, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and profile information provided during registration or through third-party login services.
  • Facebook Data: When you connect your Facebook Page, we access your Page name, category, profile picture, and Messenger conversations solely to enable messaging features within the application. We request the following Facebook permissions: pages_messaging, pages_read_engagement, pages_show_list, and pages_manage_metadata.
  • Client Data: Information you enter about your travel clients, including names, contact details, travel preferences, quotes, and booking details.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with the application.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the application's features and functionality.
  • To enable you to manage client relationships and travel bookings.
  • To facilitate messaging through connected Facebook Pages.
  • To send notifications relevant to your account and activity.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using encrypted databases. Facebook access tokens are encrypted at rest. We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Third-Party Services

We integrate with the following third-party services:

  • Facebook/Meta: For Messenger integration. Subject to Meta's Privacy Policy.

Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with our services. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Disconnect third-party services (such as Facebook) at any time through the application settings.

Data Deletion

To request deletion of your data, including any Facebook-related data, please contact us at the email address below. We will process your request within 30 days. You can also disconnect your Facebook Page at any time from within the application, which will remove all stored Facebook tokens and page data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
