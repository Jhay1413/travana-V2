CORE BUSINESS LOGIC (for UI display only)

Travel agents earn commission from bookings, but the commission is paid 8 weeks before the travel date.

This means bookings contribute to "Forwards", which represent future commission income by month.

Example:
Booking date: March 20
Travel date: December 6
Commission paid: October

Therefore the dashboard must show the next 12 months of forwards revenue.

The most important number is the total forwards commission for each month.
